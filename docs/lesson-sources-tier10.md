# Sources for structures that have no lesson yet

Checked 2026-11-15 by two research agents. Each page was opened, but through a summarising
fetch tool, so **re-read the page before writing a lesson from it**. Years are the "last updated"
year on the page. StatPearls is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0: paraphrase and cite, never copy.
211 structures lack a lesson; left/right pairs and lobule parts collapse them to about 147 ids.

## Open questions before any lesson is written

- **OpenStax licence.** The pages are CC BY-NC-SA and state they may not be used in
  generative-AI offerings without OpenStax's written permission. Existing lessons already cite
  OpenStax. Giovanni decides whether lessons drafted with AI help may rely on it; StatPearls is the
  safer default.
- **No licence confirmed** for Lehman 2020, Stoodley 2010 and Purves 2001. Cite, do not adapt.
